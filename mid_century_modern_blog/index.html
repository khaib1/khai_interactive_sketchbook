This is an interesting and challenging project. I tried to follow the instruction as much as possible but some of the part just seem off. First, finding the correct Google fonts and link them was easy. Then I changed the nav bar to match with the solution image. It looks somewhat similar, not perfect. After that, I fix the images and put them into html and write alt texts for each of them. The linking for the images was incorrect, instead of img, it was images so I changed it to the correct one. Then I follow the solution and change the font, the size of the body and headers. For the three images, I created a new class and make them in the same row. I changed the display to flex and played around with the margin and padding a little to find the best solution. However, it didn't look exactly like the solution. I also creates new class from the p in the footer so that it doesn't get mixed up with other p. Overall, I really like this project. I spend a lot of times on it to make it look good. One thing that I am curious is how do I add the organe border to the left and right of the page. I tried many things many times but still didn't work.
